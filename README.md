\# 🚀 FinPilot



\### AI-Powered Financial Investigation Agent



> FinPilot helps finance teams identify, investigate, and prioritize financial exceptions during month-end close.



\---



\## 🎯 Why FinPilot?



Month-end close often involves reviewing large numbers of transactions,

matching invoices and receipts, investigating unusual spending, and

determining which exceptions require human attention.



The challenge isn't simply calculating financial numbers.



The challenge is \*\*investigating exceptions across fragmented financial

information and turning them into actionable, evidence-backed findings.\*\*



FinPilot explores how an AI agent can assist with that investigation workflow.
